Permission defaults can now be changed from config.yml with no permissions plugin installed. Uncomment the permissions block and set any sh2 node to true, false, op or not-op. Two bundles, sh2.player and sh2.admin, move a whole role at once, and three new bypass permissions were added for admins: sh2.bypass-max-homes, sh2.bypass-blacklist and sh2.bypass-teleport-delay.
